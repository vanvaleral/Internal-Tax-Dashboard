# Management Structure

Structure is a planning-only Management tab for Supervisors, Partners and legacy
Admins. Use separate named scenarios for the existing organization, a proposed
team or a future partner. Add a Partner, Team or Role, choose its reporting line,
and optionally link an active PIC. Future roles can be left unassigned. Removing
a position moves its direct reports to the removed position's parent.

Print opens a clean A4 view of the selected saved scenario. A copy can be kept
as a PDF through the browser print dialog. Only the chart is printed.

Scenarios are saved only in the current browser, under the signed-in account's
storage key. They are not shared, backed up or stored in Supabase. Browser
storage is not an access-control boundary; use it on a trusted device and do
not enter confidential plans on a shared browser. No role, client assignment,
database record or partner workspace is created or changed.
